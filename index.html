The Studio

Interiors that feel inevitable, not decorated.

Founded in 2014, NOIR & IVORY is a design atelier working across residential and hospitality projects. We believe restraint is a form of luxury — every material, joint and beam of light is chosen with intent, so a finished room reads as effortless.

"Good design disappears. What remains is how a space makes you feel — that's the only metric I trust."

Amara VossFounder & Principal Designer
